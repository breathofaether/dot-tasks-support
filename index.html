Using .tasks

Quickly add a task

Touch and hold the .tasks app icon and choose Add task, or tap the Add task widget to open the new-task form.

Home Screen widgets

Add widgets from your Home Screen’s widget gallery. The Active tasks widget shows your total unfinished task count and up to three titles, regardless of the app’s search and filters. Tap it to open .tasks.

Widget updates may take a little time after you change a task.

Add, edit and complete

Tap + to add a task. Tap its title to edit the details, and save to keep your changes. Tap the circle beside a task to mark it complete or unfinished.

Find a task

Search matches titles and notes. Results must also match the selected status and priority. Check your filters if a task seems to be missing.

Filter and sort

Tap the filter icon beside the task count to choose a priority or sort by reminder time. Tasks without a reminder appear after dated tasks in reminder order.

Set a reminder

Turn on “Remind me,” choose a future date and allow notifications when asked. If a reminder does not appear, check notification permissions and Focus settings.

Delete tasks

Swipe a task to delete it. The gear menu also lets you delete completed tasks or all tasks, including those hidden by filters. Deletion cannot be undone in the app.

Where tasks are stored

Tasks are saved on your device. The app does not provide its own sync between devices. Device backups may include saved tasks, depending on your backup settings.

Help for .tasks on Android: tasks, categories, reminders and widgets.

Updated 10 October 2026

Need a hand?

Email rohitsaisatya.apps@proton.me. Include your Android version, device model, app version and the steps that led to the issue. Share only the task details needed to explain it.

Create and edit

Tap + to create a task. Only a title is required; notes, category, date and reminder are optional. Tap a task to edit it, then save your changes. Leaving an edited draft may ask you to discard it.

Complete and find tasks

Tap the circle to mark a task complete. Use All, Active or Completed to change what you see. Search looks in titles and notes. The sort button also contains the priority filter. Filters hide tasks; they do not delete them.

Categories

Use Manage beside the category chips to create, rename, pin or delete a category. Pin categories for quick access. Deleting a category keeps its tasks; deleting tasks in a category is a separate action.

Dates and reminders

A due date alone does not send a notification. Add a time and choose a reminder, then save. Allow notifications when prompted. Completing or deleting a task cancels its pending reminder.

If a reminder is missing

Check the task’s date, time and reminder option. In Android Settings, check .tasks notification permissions, notification channels and Alarms & reminders access where available. Do Not Disturb and battery restrictions can affect delivery or sound. Open .tasks again after force-stopping it. Delivery timing can vary by device.

Calendar

Open the calendar to browse dated tasks. Select a day to see its tasks and tap one to edit it.

Widgets and shortcuts

Long-press the app icon for Add task and Active tasks. Add widgets from your launcher’s widget picker. The Active widget shows as many tasks as fit: tap its title or count for the full list, a task to edit it, or + to add. Long-press an Active widget to resize it. Widgets follow the device theme.

Delete tasks

Use a task’s options menu to delete it, or long-press a task to select multiple tasks for deletion. Confirm carefully: deletion has no in-app undo.

Saving and errors

Tasks are saved on this device. If the app reports a loading or saving problem, follow the retry action and avoid clearing app storage. Contact support if the problem continues.

Contact

Return to Settings and choose Email support, or copy rohitsaisatya.apps@proton.me. Include what happened and the steps to reproduce it. Remove private task details from screenshots before sharing them.

First-run introduction

The three-page introduction explains tasks, organisation and reminders. Choose Next or Back to move between pages. Skip or Get started saves your choice so the introduction does not appear on every launch. You can replay it at any time from Settings → View introduction without changing your tasks.

The introduction does not request notification permission. That request appears when you enable a reminder.

Reset the app

Open Settings → Reset app and confirm only if you want to erase your local task data. Reset removes all tasks and categories, cancels reminders, refreshes widgets and restores System appearance, the Active filter and default app preferences. It also shows the introduction again.

Reset cannot be undone in the app. It does not revoke Android permissions or erase existing system backups. If reset reports an error, follow the reload or retry instructions before continuing.

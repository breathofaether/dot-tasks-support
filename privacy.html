Privacy policy for the .tasks iOS app and this support website.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who provides the app

.tasks is developed by Rohit Sai Satya Rampalli. For privacy questions or requests, email rohitsaisatya.apps@proton.me.

Task data

Task titles, notes, priorities, completion status and reminder dates are stored on your device so you can manage your tasks. The app does not send this information to the developer or a task server.

No account is required. The app includes no advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Reminders

With your permission, the app uses local notifications to remind you about tasks. Notifications can display task titles and notes. You can control notification permissions and previews in your device’s Settings.

You can turn off a task’s reminder. Deleting a task also cancels its reminder.

Widgets

The app stores a local copy of your unfinished task count and up to three task titles in storage shared with its widget extension. This information is not sent to the developer.

The Active tasks widget displays these titles wherever you place it. Anyone who can see the widget may be able to read them.

You can remove the widget at any time. Removing it does not delete your tasks. Widget updates may take a little time after task changes.

Storage, deletion and backups

Task data stays in the app’s local storage until you delete it. You can delete individual tasks, completed tasks or all tasks from within the app.

Depending on your device’s settings, saved tasks may be included in iCloud or computer backups. Deleting tasks in the app does not erase older device backups. Those backups are managed through your device and backup provider.

The app does not provide its own sync between devices.

If you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This information is used to answer your request and resolve the issue. Your email service and the developer’s email provider process the message to deliver it.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and related follow-up. You can email support to request deletion of that correspondence.

This website

This support website contains no advertising or analytics scripts and does not set its own cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as explained in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Visiting this website does not give the developer access to your tasks.

Policy changes

If the app’s data handling changes, this policy will be updated. The date above shows the latest revision.

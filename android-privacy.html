.TASKS FOR ANDROID

Privacy policy

How the .tasks Android app and this support website handle your information.

Updated 10 October 2026

Who provides the app

.tasks is developed by Rohit Sai Satya Rampalli. For privacy questions, contact rohitsaisatya.apps@proton.me.

Data stored by the app

.tasks stores task titles, notes, completion status, priorities, dates, reminder choices, categories and app preferences in its private storage on your device. It works without an account and does not provide its own cloud sync.

Collection and sharing

This version has no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. The app does not send your tasks to the developer or a developer-operated server, and the developer does not sell your task data.

Android backups

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and backup settings, app data may be included in cloud backups or transferred to another device by the operating system. These services are controlled by your device or backup provider, not by .tasks. The developer cannot access those backups. Backups are not continuous sync and are not guaranteed to contain your latest changes.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders use Android’s notification and alarm services. Notification permission lets the app show reminders; Alarms & reminders access can support more precise timing. The app also responds to device restarts to restore reminder scheduling. Task titles may appear in notifications and home-screen widgets. Use device notification settings to control visibility, including on the lock screen.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete tasks and categories inside the app, turn off individual reminders, and change notification permissions in Android Settings. Clearing the app’s storage removes its local data. Uninstalling normally removes local data, but Android may restore a backup when you reinstall. Existing backups are managed separately through your device or backup provider.

Support email

Contacting rohitsaisatya.apps@proton.me opens your email app. Sending is your choice; task data is not attached automatically. Your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are received through Proton Mail and used to respond to your support request. Your email provider and Proton Mail process the message under their own policies. You can ask about deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact rohitsaisatya.apps@proton.me. This notice describes the Android app; it will be updated when its data practices change.

Support correspondence

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and related follow-up. You can email support to request deletion of that correspondence.

This website

This support website contains no advertising or analytics scripts and does not set its own cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits under GitHub’s privacy statement. Visiting this website does not give the developer access to your tasks.
